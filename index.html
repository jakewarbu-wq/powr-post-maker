📲 Post today's ready-made post

A new post is waiting for you 4 days a week.

  1. Tap the big button Post today's ready-made post at the top of this page.
  2. Tap today's day near the top (for example Tuesday). Today's post will show.
  3. Under the pictures, tap Post to Instagram feed.
  4. A list of apps pops up. Tap Instagram. If you can't see it, slide the row of apps sideways.
  5. Choose Feed (it may say Post), then tap Next.
  6. The words are already copied for you. Press and hold in the caption box, then tap Paste.
  7. Tap Share. That's it, it's posted! 🎉
  8. Go back to the page and tick Posted, so you know it's done.

Want it in your Story too?

Do the same again, but tap Post to Instagram Story in step 3, and choose Story in step 5. Then tap Your story to share it.

📷 Make my own post

For when you have a photo of your own to share.

  1. Tap the button Make my own post at the top of this page.
  2. Tap Choose a photo and pick a photo from your phone. (You can skip this. You'll get a pretty post with just words.)
  3. Under What is this post about?, tap the one that fits, such as A class.
  4. In Headline on the image, type a few words, such as Saturday Reformer.
  5. Scroll down to Your notes. Type what you want to say in your own words. Don't worry about spelling or capital letters.
  6. Tap Write my caption. Your words are turned into a finished caption. Don't like it? Tap it again for a different one.
  7. Scroll back up and tap Post to Instagram feed. Then carry on from step 4 of the guide above.

Using a computer instead of your phone?

Tap Save feed image and Copy caption and hashtags, then post it from the Instagram app on your phone.

Stuck? Nothing you tap here can break anything, so just try again. Or ask Jake to help. 💛

Tip: add this page to your phone's home screen, so it's always one tap away.
