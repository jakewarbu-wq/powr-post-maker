Feed post

  1. Tap Save feed image above, then Copy caption and hashtags.
  2. Open Instagram. Check you are on the studio account: your profile picture at the bottom right should be the studio's. If not, press and hold it to switch accounts.
  3. Tap the + button and choose Post.
  4. Pick the POWR image from your recent photos. If it looks cut down to a square, tap the two small arrows in the bottom-left corner of the photo so the whole image shows. Tap Next.
  5. Skip the filters (the design is already finished) and tap Next.
  6. Tap Add a caption, press and hold, and choose Paste.
  7. Tap Add location and choose Clitheroe or the studio. If someone is in the photo and happy to be tagged, tap Tag people.
  8. Tap Share. Done.

Extra reach: once it's live, tap the paper-plane icon under the post and choose Add to story.

Story

  1. Tap Save Story image above. If you have a booking link, tap Copy next to it.
  2. Open Instagram, tap the + button and choose Story.
  3. Swipe up (or tap the photo square in the bottom-left) and pick the POWR Story image.
  4. Tap the sticker icon (the square smiley face) at the top. Choose Link, paste the booking link, and give it a short label such as "Book now".
  5. Drag the link sticker into the empty space under the words. Keep stickers away from the very top and bottom, where Instagram puts its own buttons.
  6. Optional: add a Location sticker for Clitheroe, or a Mention sticker for anyone featured.
  7. Tap Your story at the bottom to share it.

Stories disappear after 24 hours. To keep one on the profile, open it and tap Highlight, then add it to a highlight such as "Timetable" or "Offers".
